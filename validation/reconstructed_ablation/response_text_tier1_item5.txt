Tier 1.5 — Mobility illustration / measurable effects

We revised the mobility validation so that the safety outcome is no longer defined by the same predicate used by the runtime gate. A constraint violation is now evaluated against the true simulated inter-vehicle gap, whereas the gate receives only the noisy edge estimate. We added component ablations for freshness checking, local fallback, safety gating, and their full combination; added minimum-gap, mean-speed, jerk, and censored non-recovery metrics; and performed one-factor-at-a-time sensitivity sweeps over latency, outage duration, and deadline. Trace completeness is retained only as an auditability/configuration measure and is not interpreted as an independent performance improvement. The executable revised validation package, seed-level outputs, compressed step traces, and sensitivity summary are included in the updated validation directory.
