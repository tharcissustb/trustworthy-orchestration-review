# Eligibility criteria

## Full-text inclusion

A report is eligible for the evidence reservoir when all of the following are
confirmed from the complete text:

1. It is a substantive research, review, methods, standards, or evaluated
   systems report published within the verified review window.
2. It concerns an intelligent cyber–physical, cyber–physical–social,
   human–AI, digital-twin, edge–cloud, federated, or agentic system relevant
   to the review scope.
3. It analyzes, designs, implements, or evaluates coordination across at
   least one system interface rather than an isolated component alone.
4. It contributes evidence concerning assurance, authority, recovery,
   provenance, governance, safety, security, resilience, or reliability.

## Full-text exclusion codes

- `FT-E2`: Ineligible or non-substantive publication type.
- `FT-E3`: System or application context outside the review scope.
- `FT-E4`: Component-level contribution without an orchestration or interface
  contribution.
- `FT-E5`: No relevant assurance, authority, recovery, provenance, or
  governance evidence.
- `FT-E6`: Duplicate, superseded report, or outside the verified date window.
- `FT-E7`: Broadly relevant report that does not add a sufficiently direct,
  traceable, or non-redundant contribution to the review questions for the
  purposive evidence-rich analytical corpus.
- `FT-NR`: Complete report not retrieved; reported separately from full-text
  exclusions.

## Analytical-corpus selection

Broad topical relevance and analytical inclusion are separate decisions. A
report enters the analytical extension only if it adds at least one of:

- evaluated interface-level evidence with an identifiable method and result;
- a reproducible methodological contribution, benchmark, testbed, taxonomy,
  or systematic synthesis;
- direct evidence for an under-represented boundary, especially human
  authority, handoff, override, contestability, or recovery;
- evidence-type, domain, or temporal diversity not already represented in the
  reconciled corpus.

The study-level reason and evidence location must be recorded. Keyword
matching alone cannot determine final inclusion. Reports removed under
`FT-E7` remain visible in the screening register so that the purposive
selection is auditable.
