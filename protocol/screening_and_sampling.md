# Screening and corpus reconciliation

The review uses two linked evidence routes.

1. **Citation-led identification.** Eighty-four eligible studies were located
   through citation searching and reconciled against persistent identifiers
   and available full texts.
2. **Database identification.** Searches of IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library,
   ScienceDirect, and SpringerLink were deduplicated, screened at
   title/abstract level, and assessed at full text. Reports found by both
   routes were counted once. Thirty-six additional eligible studies were
   identified through this route.

Database searching produced 529 reports sought for retrieval. Of these,
524 were retrieved and five were not retrieved. Ten retrieved reports were
duplicates of studies already identified through citation searching.
Thirty-six additional reports met the eligibility criteria and 478 were
excluded after full-text assessment. After reconciliation across routes, the
analytical corpus comprised 120 unique studies.

The scope was bounded to support intensive study-level coding; the numerical
limit was not used as an eligibility criterion. Every included report satisfied
the substantive criteria in `eligibility_criteria.md`, and every assessed but
excluded report remains in the public decision register with one primary
reason.

The five unretrieved reports are reported separately and are not counted as
full-text exclusions.
